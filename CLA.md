# Contributor Licence Agreement

**Not legal advice.** This document is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's
Individual Contributor Licence Agreement, which is the common template for this purpose. If
you are contributing on behalf of an employer, or if anything here matters to you, have a
lawyer read it. The maintainer is not one.

## Why this exists

Replay is licensed under [Apache 2.0](LICENSE) and there is no plan to change that. This
agreement exists so that a change remains *possible* later, because that possibility expires
quietly: once a contribution lands that the maintainer has no right to relicense, the
project's licensing is fixed forever, whatever anyone later decides.

Signing costs you nothing today and keeps an option open. It does not make your contribution
proprietary, it does not remove your copyright, and it does not stop you using your own work
anywhere else.

## What you confirm

- The contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it under these
  terms.
- If your employer has rights to work you create, you have permission to contribute, or your
  employer has waived those rights.
- Your contribution does not knowingly include anyone else's code under terms incompatible
  with this agreement. If it includes third-party work, you have said so in the pull request.
- You are not obliged to provide support, and the contribution is provided without warranty
  of any kind.

## How to sign

Say so in your first pull request:

```text
I have read CLA.md and I agree to it.
Signed: Your Name <your@email>
```

One signature covers everything you contribute afterwards. If any of the facts you confirmed
above stop being true, tell the maintainer.
