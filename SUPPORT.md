# Support

Where to go for help, by kind of question.

- **Bug?** Open an issue with the bug template. Include `replay version` output and the client you were using.
- **Feature idea?** Open an issue with the feature template. Say what problem it solves before proposing a solution.
- **Question?** Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/RedRobotKK/Replay/discussions).
- **Security problem?** Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Never a public issue.

Maintainers aim to triage new issues within two working days.

**Launch week, 2026-09-14.** That is one person's aim, and this project goes to Hacker News and Product Hunt on 2026-09-14. If the week produces more issues than two working days can absorb, this line will be updated to say what is actually happening rather than left standing while it quietly stops being true. A published promise missed in silence is a worse failure here than a modest one kept, because the whole argument of this tool is that its statements carry their own date.
