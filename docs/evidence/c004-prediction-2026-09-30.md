# C004 prediction, frozen 2026-09-30 BEFORE any CLI output was examined

## Claim under attack
"Every figure Replay prints carries a truth tier: measured, estimated or
structural." README.md:293, ADR-0002.

## Boundary stated before measuring
- Unit: ADR-0002 says "the two never share a table without the label", so the
  claimed unit is the TABLE or report, not each individual digit.
- Scope: printed CLI output of the shipped binary. Not internal values.

## Deciding layer
NONE. No tier type, field or enum exists. The vocabulary is free string
literals at 15+ sites across 11 packages.

## External assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: a tier is conveyed by the words measured / estimated /
  structural. If a report conveys provenance by other wording, this
  measurement will understate compliance.
- ASSUMPTION: the fixture corpus exercises a representative report. A single
  report is not the whole printed surface.

## FROZEN PREDICTION
C004 as worded will be FALSIFIED: at least one report will print a dollar
figure with no tier word anywhere in its output.

Reason for the prediction: nothing enforces the rule. A property held by 15
scattered literals and no type is one that drifts at the first new report.

## Pre-committed consequence
- If falsified: C004's RESULT becomes REFUTED and its TEXT is narrowed to what
  the evidence supports. The test is NOT weakened to make the claim survive.
- If not falsified: C004 moves from UNRESOLVED to BOUNDED, bounded by the two
  assumptions above.
